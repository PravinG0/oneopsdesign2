PILLAR 01

API KEY VAULT

Centrally store, rotate, and monitor API keys for Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, Bedrock, Groq, Mistral, and 40+ providers. Zero keys scattered across .env files, Notion pages, and Slack threads. Expiry alerts at 14, 7, and 3 days with full audit logging.

PILLAR 02

SPEND ATTRIBUTION

Track spending by provider, model, team, and individual employee in real time. Set monthly hard budget caps and eliminate end-of-month invoice shock before it hits. Export granular chargeback reports for finance in seconds.

PILLAR 03

MODEL ASSIGNMENT

Assign specific AI models to specific teams with zero code changes. Development gets Claude and Bedrock; Marketing gets Gemini; Sales gets GPT-4o. Auto-provision via Okta/Azure AD and revoke employee access in under 30 seconds.

PILLAR 04

PRE-LLM DLP GUARDRAILS

Every prompt is scanned in real time before it reaches external LLMs. Detect 20+ PII types (SSNs, cards, medical records, client IDs) and block or redact automatically. Complete violation logs ensure instant SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR audit compliance.

PILLAR 05

TEAM CONTEXT & KB

Build a team-specific knowledge base for codebase conventions, brand guidelines, and product specs. OneOps automatically injects relevant context chunks, cutting token costs and eliminating repetitive prompt pasting across all departments.
