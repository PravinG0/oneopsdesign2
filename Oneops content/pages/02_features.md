## 2. The Cost Problem Breakdown

- **Eyebrow**: `The Problem`
- **Headline (H2)**:
  ## Nobody's watching the AI bill until it's too big to ignore.
- **Subheadline**:
  Different teams use different AI tools and AI providers, and each one has its own bill. No one adds it all up — until the finance team gets a surprise invoice and has to figure out where it came from.

### The 3 Core Friction Points:
1. **No one owns the total**
   - *Detail*: Every team pays for its own AI tools separately. There's no single number anyone can point to.
2. **Problems show up too late**
   - *Detail*: Most teams only find out they've overspent when the bill arrives, not before.
3. **AI usage is hard to track**
   - *Detail*: With teams using different tools, understanding who is spending what can be difficult. Token usage and consumption remain scattered across providers.
