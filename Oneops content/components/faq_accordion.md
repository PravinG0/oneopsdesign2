# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> Section: `Frequently Asked Questions`
> Location: Homepage (`#oneops-faq`)

### Q1: What is OneOps?
**Answer:**
OneOps is an enterprise AI governance platform that helps organizations discover, govern, optimize, and securely manage their AI ecosystem (LLMs or AI tools) such as OpenAI, Claude, Amazon Bedrock, etc. from a single dashboard. It provides complete visibility into AI tools, LLM usage, API keys, user access, spending, and compliance, enabling businesses to adopt AI confidently without sacrificing security or control.

---

### Q2: Why does my organization need an LLM observability tool like OneOps?
**Answer:**
As AI adoption grows, employees often use multiple AI tools without centralized oversight, creating security, compliance, and cost challenges. OneOps helps organizations:
- Discover all AI tools used across the enterprise
- Govern AI access with centralized policies
- Monitor AI usage and spending
- Secure API keys and credentials
- Reduce compliance and data security risks
- Scale AI adoption responsibly

This allows businesses to embrace AI innovation while maintaining enterprise-grade governance.

---

### Q3: How does OneOps work?
**Answer:**
OneOps connects your organization’s AI providers, models, and AI tools into a centralized management platform. It continuously discovers AI usage, monitors user activity, governs access permissions, tracks AI spending, and provides administrators with actionable insights through a unified dashboard. This gives IT, Security, and Leadership teams complete visibility and control over their organization’s AI ecosystem.

---

### Q4: Which AI tools and LLM providers does OneOps support?
**Answer:**
OneOps is designed to manage both enterprise AI models and widely used AI tools. It supports integration with leading LLM providers and AI tools such as:
- OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-3.5, etc.)
- Anthropic Claude (Sonnet 4, Haiku, Opus)
- Google Gemini (Gemini 1.5 Pro, Flash)
- Amazon Bedrock (Titan, Claude via Bedrock)
- Groq
- Mistral AI
- Azure OpenAI Service
- 40+ other AI APIs and foundation models

It also helps organizations monitor and govern the AI applications employees use across the enterprise, creating a centralized AI management experience.

---

### Q5: Can OneOps help organizations enforce secure AI usage policies?
**Answer:**
Yes. OneOps enables organizations to enforce AI governance policies across users, teams, and AI providers. Its built-in AI guardrails help detect sensitive information—including Personally Identifiable Information (PII)—before it is shared with AI models, ensuring employees can use AI safely while protecting critical business data and meeting compliance requirements.
